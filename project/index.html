01 / The idea

PATIENT DATA CAN TELL A STORY.
WE HELP MAKE IT CLEARER.

Re-admission can be influenced by multiple clinical and patient factors. RE:ADMIT processes structured hospital data and applies machine-learning techniques to generate a risk estimate that can support clinical decision-making.

From admission to insight
